The problem
420

rhinos were killed by poachers in South Africa in 2024, which is more than one every single day, and every one of them was killed for its horn.

499rhinos poached in South Africa in 2023
78%drop in poaching on reserves that dehorned their rhinos

Sources: South African Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment; Science, 2025.

The solution

Take away the horn, and you
take away the reason to kill.

Dehorning is painless for the rhino and the horn grows back over time, but without it a rhino is worth nothing to a poacher, which is why it is one of the most effective ways to keep them alive.

01 · AIR UNIT
HelicoptersHelicopter crews locate each rhino from the air and guide it into open ground where the team can work safely.
02 · VETERINARY
Wildlife vetThe vet darts the rhino from the helicopter and monitors its breathing and condition from sedation until it wakes.
03 · PROCEDURE
Ground teamsProcedure teams move in by vehicle, blindfold the rhino, remove the horn and treat the base before it is woken up.
04 · LOGISTICS
Ops vehiclesOperating vehicles carry the teams, equipment and fuel across the reserve so each rhino is reached quickly.
